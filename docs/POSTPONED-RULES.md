# Postponed business-rule decisions

## Confirmed Entry allocation

- Each approved PHP 1,200 Entry allocates PHP 900 to the member's held-token position and PHP 300 to the matrix.
- The PHP 900 held-token allocation is not a PHP 900 cash-payout liability in the finance report.
- A member who does not complete the three-invite qualification has a total cash-exit entitlement of PHP 693, scheduled as PHP 231 for three months.

These items are intentionally not implemented in production until the business owner confirms the authoritative behavior.

## Products Plus

- Whether eligibility starts at Exit approval time or a later purchase-cycle date.
- Exact monthly vesting boundaries and timezone.
- Whether unused purchase entitlement carries forward indefinitely.
- Whether partial claims are allowed and minimum/maximum claim amounts.
- Required proof of purchase, fulfillment workflow, refunds, and rejected-claim behavior.
- Whether the percentage is a product bonus, discount, credit, or cash-equivalent benefit.

## Exit payments

- Required payment evidence for each invest/reinvest action.
- Whether admin must record a verified transaction reference before approval.
- Refund/cancellation rules after rejection or approval.
- Whether a rejected Exit request may reuse the same payment reference.

## Passive income

- Exact release time and timezone for monthly income.
- Treatment of weekends, holidays, suspended accounts, reversals, and rounding.
- Whether approval dates or fixed calendar dates anchor future monthly releases.

## Withdrawals

- Minimum and maximum withdrawal amounts.
- Fees, processing schedule, cutoff times, and daily/monthly limits.
- Whether members may cancel pending requests.
- Required identity/KYC verification and handling of failed GCash payouts.

## Matrix governance

- Whether administrators may move an already placed member.
- Whether sponsorship can ever change after registration.
- Treatment of deleted, suspended, refunded, or duplicate accounts.
- Formal dispute and manual-correction procedure.
