## Accepted limitations and separate sign-off

- Supabase Auth's leaked-password protection is Pro-only and disabled on the current Free project. The owner explicitly accepted this Free-plan warning for now. This is not a passed security control; app-side password rules are only a partial safeguard.
- F3 is a standalone token app. The provided sample wallet IDs have `0x` plus 40 hexadecimal characters, but the owner asked to defer network-specific handling. Token delivery remains manual and must verify the destination outside the site; no blockchain network is assumed.
- Owner Finances remains a limited historical view, not a complete cash/liability report. It must not be used as the final financial reconciliation source.
- Independent legal review of the token, monthly income, and investment offers was explicitly deferred. Technical readiness does not authorize public financial enrollment or promotion.
